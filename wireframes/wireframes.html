Design notes

  • Catalog first: standard items carry pre-approved specifications, which removes the unclear-specification rework found in elicitation (EF-07).
  • Mandatory fields are validated inline; an incomplete PR cannot be submitted.
  • Fast-track is visible but deliberately frictioned: selecting it requires a justification and warns that post-review will follow (FR-08).
  • Form autosaves on connection loss (NFR-04); target completion time under 5 minutes (US-01).

Design notes

  • Everything needed for a decision is on one screen: items, value, budget impact, priority. No attachments to hunt through.
  • Designed for a phone browser first, because approvers are on site rounds (NFR-04).
  • Reminder at 24 hours, delegation after the configured period; both events land in the audit trail (UAT-02).
  • Rejection without a reason is blocked by design (UAT-03).

Design notes

  • This screen is the direct answer to the top elicitation finding: "We never know where our PR is" (EF-01).
  • Stage SLA breaches are highlighted with the escalation state, so a stalled PR is visible before it becomes a phone call.
  • Expected PO and delivery dates are estimates from stage targets, labeled as estimates.
