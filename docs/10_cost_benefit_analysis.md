**ProcureBA, EPC Procurement Digitalization Case Study**

> Every figure in this document is a case-study estimate. Cost figures are planning estimates for the recommended SaaS option (see Build vs Buy Analysis). Benefit baselines are computed from the synthetic dataset analysis; benefit realization rates are stated assumptions, not measured results.

## 2. Quantified Benefits (annual, at steady state)

| ID | Benefit | Baseline from analysis | Assumption | Annual benefit (Rp) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| BEN-1 | Maverick price premium avoided | Excess spend from maverick buying is Rp 8,374,710,381 over 2 years, about Rp 4.19 billion per year (10.8% premium on 12.2% of PRs) | Fast-track lane and deadline discipline eliminate 70% of the excess; 30% of genuine urgencies still pay some premium | 2,930,000,000 |
| BEN-2 | Better prices on planned purchases through structured competition | Planned (non-maverick) spend is about Rp 283.33 billion per year (total spend Rp 656.10 billion minus maverick Rp 89.44 billion, divided by 2 years) | Deadline-driven RFQs with at least 3 comparable quotations improve prices by 0.5% on planned spend | 1,420,000,000 |
| BEN-3 | Procurement administration time saved | 8 procurement staff spend an estimated 30% of their time on retyping, filing, and status chasing | Loaded cost Rp 12,000,000 per person per month; the 30% manual share is removed by the system | 350,000,000 |

**Benefits deliberately not quantified:** reduced project delay risk from late materials, audit quality improvement, and requester time saved across five sites (504 urgent PRs and thousands of status inquiries). These are real but are excluded to keep the case conservative.

## 3. Three-Year Summary

Year 1 benefits are counted at 50% of steady state because the rollout completes in phases (see Solution Roadmap).

| | Year 1 (Rp) | Year 2 (Rp) | Year 3 (Rp) | Total (Rp) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Benefits | 2,430,000,000 | 4,850,000,000 | 4,850,000,000 | 12,130,000,000 |
| One-time costs | 800,000,000 | 0 | 0 | 800,000,000 |
| Recurring costs | 540,000,000 | 540,000,000 | 540,000,000 | 1,620,000,000 |
| **Net** | **1,090,000,000** | **4,310,000,000** | **4,310,000,000** | **9,710,000,000** |

- **Three-year ROI:** (12,130,000,000 − 2,420,000,000) / 2,420,000,000 = **about 401%**.
- **Payback:** cumulative net turns positive during Year 1; with the phased benefit ramp, payback is approximately **9 months from project start** (estimate).
- **Break-even benefit level:** the project stays net positive over three years even if only about 20% of the projected benefits materialize, because recurring costs (Rp 0.54 billion per year) are small relative to the maverick excess spend baseline alone (Rp 4.19 billion per year).

## 4. Sensitivity Notes

| Scenario | Change | Three-year net (Rp) |
|---|---|---|
| Base case | As above | 9,710,000,000 |
| Conservative benefits | All benefit lines at 50% of estimate, full realization delayed to Year 2 | about 3,600,000,000 |
| Maverick reduction disappoints | BEN-1 at 35% elimination instead of 70% | about 6,800,000,000 |
| Subscription cost doubles | Recurring Rp 1.08 billion per year | about 8,100,000,000 |

The case does not depend on any single benefit line: BEN-1 alone, at the stated 70% realization, covers all three-year costs by itself.
