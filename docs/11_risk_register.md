# Risk Register

**ProcureBA, EPC Procurement Digitalization Case Study**
Probability and impact are rated Low, Medium, or High for the fictional case company. Owner refers to the stakeholder IDs in `docs/02_stakeholder_analysis.md`.

| ID | Risk | Cause | Probability | Impact | Mitigation | Contingency | Owner |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| RSK-01 | Approvers resist replacing wet signatures with e-approval | Habit, perceived loss of control, legal uncertainty | High | High | Company decree on electronic approvals before Phase 1; mobile approval design; sponsor visibly approves in the system from day one | Keep a parallel paper approval for the first month at pilot sites only, with a fixed end date | SH-01 |
| RSK-02 | Vendors do not adopt the quotation portal | Low IT capability, especially Tier C vendors; change fatigue | Medium | High | Assisted onboarding and first quotation done together; portal limited to one simple entry page; email remains the notification channel; helpdesk during RFQ windows | Procurement enters quotations on behalf of a vendor with the vendor's written confirmation, flagged in the audit log, for the first two cycles only | SH-02 |
| RSK-03 | Sites continue maverick buying after go-live | Fast official process still feels slower than a phone call; habit | High | High | Fast-track lane designed with Project Managers; reimbursement rule requires a system record; monthly maverick report by site to the steering committee | Escalate repeat sites to the Director of Operations; review whether fast-track SLAs are actually being met | SH-01, SH-03 |
| RSK-04 | Low bandwidth at remote sites makes the system frustrating | Site connectivity limits | Medium | Medium | NFR-04 as a contractual acceptance item tested from the most remote site; lightweight pages; form autosave | Provide a structured email fallback form that procurement keys in, visibly flagged, while connectivity is fixed | SH-09 |
| RSK-05 | Vendor master data migration errors (bank accounts, tax IDs) | Years of spreadsheet records with duplicates and typos | Medium | High | Dual verification against finance payment records; second-person check on bank fields; pilot payments to the top 5 vendors before full cutover | Roll back to the verified pre-migration snapshot; freeze vendor payments for affected records only | SH-04 |
| RSK-06 | Scope creep toward a full ERP program | Stakeholders attach unrelated finance and inventory wishes | Medium | Medium | Phase gates with explicit out-of-scope list from the BRD; change requests priced and deferred to a future phase decision | Steering committee re-baselines the roadmap; procurement scope protected as the first value milestone | SH-01 |
| RSK-07 | Peak season (March to August) collides with rollout | Seasonal PR volume is 2.07 times the low month | Medium | Medium | Schedule Phase 1 go-live outside the peak window; pilot in a low-volume month; seasonal capacity plan in Phase 3 | Temporary additional procurement staff during the first peak after go-live | SH-02 |
| RSK-08 | Catalog standardization weakens technical specifications | Over-simplified catalog items for complex equipment | Low | High | QA/QC and HSE review of catalog specifications before go-live (SH-10); non-catalog free-text path remains available with procurement validation | Revert specific categories to validated free-text until specifications are corrected | SH-10 |
| RSK-09 | Benefits are not realized because stage targets are not enforced | SLA timers exist but nobody acts on escalations | Medium | High | Monthly KPI review with the steering committee using the dashboard; stage owners named per SLA; maverick and cycle targets in the Procurement Manager's objectives | Process audit after the first quarter; adjust staffing or rules where the data shows slippage | SH-02 |
| RSK-10 | Audit gap during transition between paper and digital records | Two record systems run in parallel during cutover | Low | Medium | Cutover plan documents which system is authoritative per site and date; paper archive indexed and retained per NFR-05; auditors informed in advance (SH-11) | Extend dual running for one additional month at any site where the archive index is incomplete | SH-04 |

## Risk Review Cadence

- Risks RSK-01, RSK-02, and RSK-03 are reviewed at every steering committee meeting during Phases 1 and 2 because they are behavior risks, not technology risks.
- The register is re-scored at each phase gate; closed risks remain visible with their closure evidence (for example, the first month of maverick rate data for RSK-03).
