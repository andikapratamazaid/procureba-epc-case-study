# Change Management and Training Plan

**ProcureBA, EPC Procurement Digitalization Case Study**

## 1. Change Story

The change is framed in one sentence for every audience: **"Same procurement, no paper chase: every PR visible, every approval in one tap, every urgent need with a legitimate fast lane."** Communication never leads with the system brand name; it leads with the pain each audience already feels (see Elicitation Pack findings EF-01 to EF-08).

## 2. Impacted User Groups

| Group | Size (case estimate) | Impact level | What changes for them |
|---|---|---|---|
| Site Engineers and Supervisors (requesters) | about 60 | High | Paper forms replaced by catalog PRs; phone status checks replaced by the tracking page; maverick buying replaced by the fast-track lane |
| Project Managers (approvers) | 5 plus delegates | High | Wet signatures replaced by in-app approval; delegation rule formalized |
| Procurement Officers | 8 | High | Spreadsheet and retyping replaced by queue, RFQ automation, and comparison matrix; role shifts toward vendor management |
| Procurement Manager | 1 | High | Owns new KPIs and monthly reporting rhythm |
| Warehouse Staff | about 15 | Medium | Paper delivery notes replaced by in-system goods receipt |
| Finance Staff | about 10 | Medium | Manual matching replaced by exception handling |
| Vendors | 18 companies | Medium | Email quotations replaced by portal entry; deadlines enforced |
| IT and System Admin | 3 | Medium | New system to administer, SSO and access reviews |
| Management and Directors | about 10 | Low | Receive dashboards instead of ad hoc reports |

## 3. Communication Plan

| When | Audience | Message | Channel | Owner |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Month 0 | All staff | Why procurement is changing: the measured problems (17.5 day average cycle, 12.2% maverick) and what will improve | Director of Operations announcement plus site toolbox talks | SH-01 |
| Month 0 | Project Managers | Approval workflow, delegation rule, and fast-track governance design session | Workshop | BA and SH-02 |
| Month 1 | Requesters at pilot sites | How to raise a catalog PR and track it; who the site champions are | Champion-led briefings and a one-page quick guide | Site champions |
| Month 2 | All sites | Pilot results (measured stage times before and after), go-live schedule per site | Dashboard snapshot shared by email and notice boards | SH-02 |
| Month 4 | Vendors | Portal onboarding: how quotations, deadlines, and acknowledgments work | Vendor session (online) plus assisted first quotation | SH-02 |
| Month 5 | All staff | Fast-track lane is live; off-system buying will no longer be reimbursed without a system record | Finance and Procurement joint notice | SH-04, SH-02 |
| Monthly | Management | KPI dashboard review: cycle time, maverick rate, on-time rate, queue aging | Steering committee | SH-02 |

## 4. Resistance Management

| Expected resistance | Source | Response |
|---|---|---|
| "Phone calls are faster than any system." | Project Managers, senior engineers | Acknowledge the truth in it today: that is exactly the maverick problem. Offer the fast-track lane as the legitimate version of the phone call, with a 4-hour approval SLA, and publish fast-track performance monthly |
| "The system is surveillance of procurement." | Procurement Officers | Involve officers in queue and KPI design; first dashboard review is internal to procurement before it goes to management |
| "Our vendors cannot use a portal." | Procurement, vendors | Assisted onboarding, one-page quotation entry, email-triggered access, helpdesk during RFQ windows; measure vendor submission success and report it |
| "Site internet will make this unusable." | Site teams | Pilot deliberately includes the most remote site; NFR-04 acceptance test from that site before rollout is approved |

## 5. Training Plan

| Audience | Format | Duration | Timing | Content |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Requesters (Site Engineers, Supervisors) | Hands-on session at site plus quick guide | 2 hours | One week before their site goes live | Catalog PR submission, attachments, tracking page, fast-track rules |
| Project Managers and delegates | Hands-on session | 1.5 hours | Before Phase 1 pilot | Mobile approval, delegation setup, clarification and rejection handling, dashboard basics |
| Procurement Officers | Classroom plus sandbox practice with the UAT scripts | 2 days | Before Phase 1 pilot, refresher before Phase 2 | Queue management, RFQ generation, vendor portal support, comparison and award, e-PO, scorecard reading |
| Procurement Manager | Coaching sessions | 3 sessions of 2 hours | Monthly during rollout | KPI review rhythm, vendor review meetings, escalation handling |
| Warehouse Staff | Hands-on session at site | 2 hours | Before Phase 2 | Goods receipt recording, partial deliveries, mismatch handling |
| Finance Staff | Hands-on session | 3 hours | Before Phase 2 | Three-way match exceptions, reporting, audit retrieval |
| Vendors | Online session plus assisted first quotation | 1 hour | Before their first live RFQ | Portal sign-in, quotation entry, deadline rules, PO acknowledgment |
| System Admin (IT) | Vendor technical training | 2 days | Before Phase 1 | User and role administration, approval matrix, catalog maintenance, backup and audit log review |

## 6. Adoption Measures

Adoption is measured, not assumed. The steering committee reviews monthly: percentage of PRs raised digitally (target 100% from each site's go-live), fast-track share and post-review completion, maverick purchases detected through finance (target 3% or less of PRs by Phase 3), average stage times against the to-be targets, and vendor portal submission rate (target 90% or more of quotations submitted through the portal by Phase 3).
